Use Case Description

  • Banks put AI assistants in front of the underwriting referral queue. The assistant writes the summary a human underwriter decides from. Nobody can prove those summaries are safe.
  • We generate the loan applications ourselves, so the right answer is known before the assistant sees the case. Deterministic checks grade the briefing; a judge grades readability only.
  • Output: an evidence pack mapped to EU AI Act Articles 14 and 15, every figure traceable and re-verifiable.

Challenge(s)

  • A summary can be accurate, readable and dangerous — because of what it leaves out. No existing tool catches omissions.
  • The judge is not enough. Nemotron 3 Ultra scored an incomplete briefing 2/2. Our omission check failed it.
  • RAG fails quietly. Retrieval ranked policy text over the applicant's numbers; the assistant fabricated them. We must tell retrieval failure from model failure.
  • Every figure in the evidence must trace to its source and survive a re-check a year later.
  • Evidence Articles 14 and 15 honestly — and name what we do not cover.
