The problem: a briefing that reads well and is wrong

One referred loan. The assistant's summary, the judge model's grade, and the real number.
APP000044 · Nemotron 3.5 Lightning · our GPU · 16 Sep 2026
“…the total monthly debt service works out to approximately £630 per month. As a percentage of the applicant's gross monthly income (£1,327), this results in a TMDS ratio of 41.58%. This exceeds the policy threshold of 40%.”
Judge · Nemotron 3 Ultra
2 / 2
Intelligible. Actionable.
Case file · arithmetic
47.5%
£630 ÷ £1,326.83. The stated ratio appears nowhere in the file.
  • Banks are putting assistants like this in front of the underwriting referral queue. The underwriter decides from the summary.
  • Ten runs of this case, same settings: ten different ratios, eight of them wrong. Six cited the applicant's age band as a factor. It has no weight in the decision.
  • The judge model gave every one of them full marks. A model cannot tell you what another model got wrong.
  • Under the EU AI Act this is a high-risk system (Annex III 5(b)). The deployer must show the human could see what mattered. Today, nobody can.

Why existing approaches miss it — and what we do instead

Verifier's Law (Shafiq, Wells Fargo): automate what is hard to do but easy to verify.→Make the right answer known before the model runs, and every check becomes a comparison, not an opinion.
Generic LLM evaluation (DeepEval, Ragas, LLM-as-judge)Credit Evidence Engine
Where the right answer comes fromSomeone labels it, or a judge model decidesComputed from a scorecard on generated cases, before any model call
What a score means“Faithfulness 0.83” — a model's opinion, averaged“The briefing omitted the 40% limit in 3 of 20 cases” — checkable by reading the file

Ground truth by construction

We generate the loan applications, so we know what drove each decision before the assistant reads the file.
1 · hidden
Repayment-capacity tier
A latent variable the generator uses and then drops. It never appears in any document.
→
2 · observed
The case file
Income, commitments, bureau score, delinquencies, file age — derived from the tier. Plus decoys: age band, dependants, postcode, employer — generated with no path to the outcome.
→
3 · decided
Scorecard
Seven weighted fields, one policy limit (40% debt service). Approve, refer or decline. Decoys weigh zero.
→
4 · sealed
Marking key
For every referred case: the facts that drove it, the numbers, the decoys, what would flip it. The assistant never sees this.
700applications generated by Synthetic Data Designer from one YAML spec
20referred cases in the pack, three documents each: application, bureau report, lending policy
r ≈ −0.04correlation of a decoy with the outcome, measured — versus +0.72 for a real driver
0helper columns in the case file. Tested: the hidden tier does not leak

What we found so far real · 16 Sep · to be updated with the full run

8 / 10runs of one case stated a debt-service ratio not in the file. The judge passed all ten. Only a numeric check catches it.
6 / 10cited the applicant's age band as a factor against them. It has zero weight and is a protected characteristic.
10 / 10distinct outputs at temperature 0 with a fixed seed — on the cloud endpoint and on our own vLLM. Reproducibility must be defined as “same verdict across N runs”.
2–3 sper briefing on our RTX PRO 6000 node, against 8–150 s on the shared cloud endpoint. Same code; two lines of configuration.
  • Retrieval failed on the first run: the question reads like a policy question, so the retriever ranked the policy sections above the data, and the assistant wrote a confident briefing with numbers from nowhere. The transcript records what was retrieved, so the failure can be attributed.
  • The omission check alone is not enough: a briefing can state the breach and still fabricate the ratio. No single check is sufficient; the pack runs all of them.

Who uses it, and why it lasts

Public sector
  • The reader of an evidence pack is a supervisor — ECB, BaFin, FCA, the Federal Reserve. The pack is written for them.
  • The same assistant pattern runs inside public lenders and schemes: state development banks, government-guaranteed SME and student loans, housing authorities.
  • Annex III 5(b) covers any creditworthiness assessment of a person — not only by banks.
Viability
  • Apache 2.0. One complete sample pack ships with the code.
  • Runs where the data is. Cloud or on-prem, two lines of configuration; nothing leaves the site.
  • A new domain is a new spec and scorecard. The checks, the pack and the verifier do not change.
  • Regulations change without retraining. They are documents in a retrieval index.
  • Runs in a harness a bank may already have — NeMo Evaluator, with pass/fail gates for continuous assessment.
What we do not claim:the loan decision itself (verified by the loan book, years later)·compliance (we evidence; a reviewer judges)·fairness across a population
Evidence, not opinion.
The right answer is computed before the model runs. Every check is a comparison. The judge grades readability and nothing else. The pack can be re-verified by anyone, years later.
