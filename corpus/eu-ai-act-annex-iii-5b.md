title: EU AI Act, Annex III point 5(b) — creditworthiness (summary)
cites: ["Annex III"]
version: "Regulation (EU) 2024/1689"
---
Summary for testing, not the legal text. AI systems intended to evaluate the
creditworthiness of natural persons or establish their credit score are listed
as high-risk, with an exception for systems used to detect financial fraud.
