title: Consumer Credit Directive (EU) 2023/2225, Article 18 — Creditworthiness assessment (summary)
cites: ["Article 18", "Art. 18", "Art 18"]
version: "Directive (EU) 2023/2225"
---
Summary for testing, not the legal text. Before concluding a credit agreement the
creditor must thoroughly assess the consumer's creditworthiness on the basis of
relevant and accurate information on income and expenses, and the consumer has
the right to a human review where automated processing is used.
