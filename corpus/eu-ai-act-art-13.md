cites: ["Article 13", "Art. 13", "Art 13"]
version: "Regulation (EU) 2024/1689"
---
Summary for testing, not the legal text. High-risk systems must be designed so
deployers can interpret their output and use it appropriately, and must come with
instructions for use that describe capabilities and limitations.
