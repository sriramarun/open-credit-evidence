title: Lending policy PL-2026.1 — Affordability
cites: ["PL-2026.1", "affordability rule"]
version: "PL-2026.1"
---
Total monthly debt service — existing credit commitments plus the proposed
instalment — must not exceed 40% of gross monthly income. Applications above the
threshold require underwriter review. The underwriter may consider a reduced
facility, a longer term, or additional verified income.
